1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Ludo – Online Multiplayer ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles information when you use our mobile and web application. We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring full compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play Data Safety policies, and international privacy standards.

2. Information Collection and Use

We practice strict data minimization. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share sensitive personal information.

  • No Account Credentials: No email, password, phone number, or OTP is ever requested or recorded.
  • Anonymous Guest Identifier: When you open the Game, a randomized, anonymous Guest Player ID (such as SL-986881) is automatically generated and saved locally on your device storage. This identifier is used exclusively to keep track of your match statistics (wins, losses, streaks) and allow friends to challenge you.
  • Player Nickname & Avatar: You may choose a non-personal display name and an icon avatar. This display name is stored locally on your device and transmitted ephemerally to peers only during active multiplayer sessions.
  • No Precise Location Data: We do not access or track GPS coordinates or location information.
  • No Financial Data: The game does not collect payment cards, banking info, or billing addresses.

3. Real-Time Multiplayer and Data Safety

When participating in online multiplayer (Quick Match, Room Code, or Direct Challenge):

  • Your chosen nickname, avatar, and anonymous Player ID are visible to connected players in the game room.
  • Game actions (dice rolls, token positions) are synchronized between players via secure WebSockets to ensure fair play and prevent cheating.
  • When a match concludes, match logs and scores are saved in your device's sandboxed local storage.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

Smart Ludo is designed as a family-friendly board game suitable for players of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The game does not sell personal user data to data brokers. Any platform-level analytics or crash reporting services operate anonymously to maintain app stability and performance.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All game progress, statistics, and guest identification reside locally on your device. You can completely erase all stored data and reset your guest identifier at any time by clearing the application's data or cache through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Smart Ludo > Storage > Clear Storage/Data).

7. Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/WSS) for all online network communication. Server-side dice roll generation ensures that game mechanics remain tamper-proof.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated effective date.
